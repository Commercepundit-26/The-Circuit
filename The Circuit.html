09

Vertical Connectivity System

The Current — The Central Staircase

Transforming the primary stairwell from an overlooked utility into a dynamic, wellness-driven architectural spine connecting all five floors.

Landing Color-Morph Sequence

Each landing wall features a clean transitional mural that visually blends the color of the floor below into the color of the floor above as you climb.

Landing 3rd → 4th: The Nexus to The Bridge Sherpa Blue → Warm Bronze / Slate
Landing 2nd → 3rd: Nexus Tech Continuum Concrete Black → Bittersweet Red
Landing 1st → 2nd: The Ignite to The Nexus Kitchen Orange → CommercePundit Monochrome
Ground → 1st: The Origin to The Ignite Workforce Blue → Kitchen Orange
Integrated Wellness Runner: Along the stair skirting and risers runs an active step-count and calorie milestone metric (e.g., "Step 84 • Climbing Higher • 12.8 kcal burned") encouraging active stair usage over elevator trips.
Architectural Intent
By providing continuous visual progress markers, the stairwell becomes a symbol of personal and collective momentum. Team members experience the physical flow of The Circuit every time they move between meetings.
Mid-Landing Typography
Clean directional signage and level identifiers at every landing ("Level 01 • The Ignite", "Level 02 • The Nexus") ensure effortless orientation for team members and guests alike.
