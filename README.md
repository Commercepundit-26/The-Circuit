# The Circuit — Atlanta HQ Workplace Identity

An interactive architectural presentation and environmental graphic design system for the multi-floor Atlanta Headquarters.

## Overview

"The Circuit" connects all five floors of the Atlanta Headquarters into a cohesive spatial narrative, physically and culturally linked by the central staircase ("The Current").

### Building Narrative & Floors

1. **Ground Floor — The Origin** (Workforce247)
   - *Colors*: Workforce247 Blue (`#1E5FFF`), Deep Navy (`#0A1128`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
   - *Rooms*: The Discover, The Thrive, The Base, The Root
   - *Scope*: Reception welcome wall vinyl, HR interview frosted manifestation

2. **1st Floor — The Ignite** (Kitchen365)
   - *Colors*: Kitchen Orange (`#FF7A29`), Charcoal Jet (`#222222`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`)
   - *Rooms*: The Kindle, The Forge
   - *Scope*: Open bay graphics, conference glass film manifestation

3. **2nd & 3rd Floors — The Nexus** (CommercePundit)
   - *Primary Palette*: Concrete (`#F2F2F2`), White (`#FFFFFF`), Black (`#000000`)
   - *Secondary Palette*: Bittersweet (`#FC766A`), Morning Glory (`#95E2E2`), Sherpa Blue (`#005463`)
   - *Rooms*: The Node, The Link, The Core, The Path, The Grid, The Mesh, The Loop
   - *Scope*: Acoustic canvas column wraps, calling pod nooks, meeting room manifestation

4. **4th Floor — The Bridge** (Executive & Client Governance)
   - *Colors*: Alabaster (`#F8F6F0`), Graphite (`#2D2D2D`), Warm Bronze (`#8C7355`), Subtle Slate (`#E2E6EA`)
   - *Rooms*: The Atlanta (Boardroom), The Anchor (Leadership), The Horizon (Client Presentation)
   - *Scope*: Refined architectural matte finishes, tactile privacy manifestation

5. **Central Staircase — The Current**
   - *Colors*: Four transitional color-morph landings
   - *Features*: Step-count & calorie runner along the stair skirting

## Interactive Presentation

Open [`The Circuit.html`](./The%20Circuit.html) in any modern web browser to view the interactive presentation deck:
- High-contrast Swiss architectural monochrome layout
- Slide Overview drawer (`O` key)
- Mockup Showcase gallery with full-resolution Lightbox inspection
- Click-to-copy color swatches
- Native keyboard navigation (`←`, `→`, `Space`, `F` for fullscreen, `Esc` to close modal/drawer)
